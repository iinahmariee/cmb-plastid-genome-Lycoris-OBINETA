## Main structural features observed
The *Lycoris radiata* plastid genome exhibits the typical angiosperm plastid genome quadripartite architecture, consisting of a large single-copy (LSC) region, a small single-copy (SSC) region, and two inverted repeat regions designated IRa and IRb. The IR regions are identical in sequence and separate the LSC from the SSC. The genome contains the full complement of expected genes, including those for photosystem I and II subunits, ATP synthase components, ribosomal proteins, ribosomal RNAs, and transfer RNAs. Gene distribution is uneven across the genome; the LSC carries the greatest number of unique genes, while the IR regions show high sequence conservation and duplicate the genes located within them. No large-scale structural rearrangements or major gene losses were detected relative to other sequenced angiosperm plastid genomes.
